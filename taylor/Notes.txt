The full RMS difference is resolved into these two distinct components (overall bias $\bar{E}$ and pattern difference $E'$) which add together quadratically: $E^2 = \bar{E}^2 + {E'}^2$
RMSE in the taylor diagram is actualy pattern difference RMSE (bias corrected rmse)

We don't supply RMSE at all to add_model_set in gv taylor diagram. To locate point on a taylor diagram model std and correlation are enough.

The std is either normalized std or raw std. We don't need to do normalization of model std if we plotting parameters with same units
